First time? Dropbox app setup
  1. Open dropbox.com/developers/apps and open your app (or create one: Scoped access, Full Dropbox).
  2. Permissions tab: tick files.metadata.read, files.content.read, files.content.write. Tap Submit.
  3. Settings tab, Redirect URIs: add and tap Add.
  4. Copy the App key from the Settings tab into the box above.
